Team Context

Loading slate…

How worn down each MLB team is going into a game: rest, recent workload, road trips, when they next get a day off or travel, and how often today's starting lineup has played lately. Ported from BettingEdge. Doesn't feed the MLB model on Models; it's a situational read to weigh alongside it.

Fatigue scores schedule load from 0 to 100: no day off (+25, or +10 for one), games in the last 3 days (up to +22) and 7 days (up to +22), a doubleheader today (+22), playing yesterday (+10, +18 after a doubleheader), and 3+ or 6+ straight road games (+8 / +16). fresh under 25, elevated 25–44, stressed 45–69, burnt 70+. A Rest edge flag marks a fresh team facing a burnt one with at least 2 more days of rest.
