Fair % is the market's implied win probability with the sportsbook's built-in margin (the “vig”) removed, so both teams' numbers add up to 100%. Model % is this site's own from-scratch estimate, computed from MLB stats (runs scored/allowed, adjusted for that day's probable starting pitchers) — it has no idea what the market thinks. Edge is Model % − Fair %: how much more (or less) the model likes a team than the market does.

Value = model favors this side by ≥ 4 points more than the market — worth a second look. Fade = model favors them ≥ 4 points less. Check = model and market disagree by ≥ 10 points, which usually means the model is missing something (stale or thin pitcher data) rather than a real edge — a flag to double-check, not a pick. No tag = model and market roughly agree, nothing actionable. Each row's second line explains the specific reasoning behind its call. Full breakdown of the sources and math: how the model works.
